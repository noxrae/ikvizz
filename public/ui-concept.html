Built on Transformative Teal — WGSN's official 2026 colour, meaning change + stability. Fresh, futuristic, and made for a relationships app. Plus a fully custom icon language, hand-drawn for every single feature.

One confident signature — deep teal — lifted by a mint glow and a warm amber spark. Calm base, energetic accents.

Why teal? WGSN named Transformative Teal the 2026 Colour of the Year — "a fluid fusion of dependable blue and aquatic green… for a period of change and redirection." For an app about evolving relationships, that meaning is the brand. It's also nothing like the usual chat-app blue/green — it feels current, premium, and unmistakably yours.
