Not flat. Not another dark theme. Soft Matter is a living material — liquid glass that refracts light, meets clay that squishes when you press it. Every message is a soft 3D pebble. Every friend has a pearl that's alive. Depth you can feel, not just see.

01
The material

Two 2026 materials fused: Apple's Liquid Glass (depth, refraction) + Claymorphism (soft, tactile 3D). Hover them — they respond.

Liquid Clay 🫧

Buttons that look pressable — puffy, matte, with inner + outer shadow. Spring-bounce on tap. Your brain reads them faster than a flat rectangle.

Liquid Glass 🔮

Translucent panels that let light and colour bend through. They float above content and recede so your words stay the hero.

03
The moves nobody else has
🔮

The Vibe Pearl

Each friend is a 3D pearl that shifts colour with their mood and grows brighter as your Relationship DNA levels up. Your avatar, alive.

👆

Squish to react

Press a message and the reaction bubbles physically squish and pop with spring physics — a tiny hit of joy every tap.

📱

Real depth

Tilt the phone and glass layers parallax over the clay beneath. The interface has a floor, a mid, and a top — like a real object.

🌊

Liquid motion

Nothing snaps. Panels melt in, bubbles settle with weight, the send button ripples. Buttery, weighty, alive.
