04
Why it works
🌙

Dark, but warm

Space-ink is easy on the eyes at 2am, and the aurora keeps it from feeling cold or corporate.

👆

Thumb-first

Big tap targets, bottom-anchored actions, long-press for power moves — built for one hand on a phone.

🎭

Loud where it counts

Reactions, streaks and "your" bubbles pop; everything else stays quiet so the chat breathes.

⚡

Same app, zero relearning

Every feature stays exactly where it is. This is a new skin, not a new map.
